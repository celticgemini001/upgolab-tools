Upscaling anime and line art without ruining the lines

Line art needs the opposite treatment of a photograph. A photo model sees a flat fill as missing detail and fills it in. Here is what to do instead.

Why generic upscalers fail on anime

Photo models are trained on grain, pores and texture. Feed them a cel-shaded frame and they decide the flat colour is impoverished, so they add shading that the artist never drew and soften hard strokes into gradients. The result is a blobby frame that reads as "AI processed" at a glance.

The fix is not a stronger setting. It is a different model class, trained on flat fills and hard edges, plus a lighter hand overall.

What to skip

SettingOn anime
Texture or grain preservationOff. It adds speckle to flat colour
Aggressive denoiseOnly for block artifacts. Full-frame denoise blotches fills
Face restorationUsually too photographic, over-defines anime eyes
Sharpening after upscaleLight only. It amplifies the noise in the fills

The order

  1. Denoise compression blocks only
  2. Enlarge at 2x per axis, 2D preset
  3. Check a line edge at 100%
  4. Stop. Do not grade after
Re-export beats upscaling when you can. If you still have the source project, most editors export vector or at least a larger raster. Upscaling is for the frame you only have as a JPEG.

JPEG damage sits exactly on the lines

Block boundaries land on hard edges, which is the worst place for them. Enlarge without removing the grid and the strokes thicken with the artifact baked in.

Compression noise is not grain. The grid is regular, grain is random. Denoise for the grid specifically, then enlarge.

Frequently asked

Why does AI upscaling ruin anime line art?

Photo models treat flat fills as missing detail, so they add grain and shading, and they soften hard strokes into gradients. A 2D preset is trained on the opposite.

Should you denoise anime before upscaling?

Only the compression artifacts. Aggressive full-frame denoise blotches flat colour.

What upscale factor is safe for line art?

2x per axis at most. Past that line weights go uneven and text turns into scribbles.

Does JPEG compression matter on anime frames?

Yes, badly. Block boundaries sit on the line edges and get enlarged along with them.
